EEA stands for Electronics, Electrical Energy, and Automation.

At the University of Montpellier, it is a two-year master's degree (Bac+5) combining theory, practical projects, research, and internships.

Basically, it's a master's in electrical and electronic engineering, focused on creating smart technologies. We learn how electronic devices, machines, robots, and energy systems work, and how to design and automate them. It prepares us to develop technology used in modern industries.

Programming was also part the education program. The degree provides a good foundation for embedded software, automation, robotics, and control engineering. However, it does not necessarily cover the same depth of web development, cloud computing, or application architecture as a dedicated computer science master's
